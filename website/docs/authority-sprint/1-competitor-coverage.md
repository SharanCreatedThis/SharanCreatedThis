## The finding that matters most

**Lucky Dangle sells 12 cultural charms for $7.77–$11.11. Eleven of them are charms Hangly
already ships for free.**

Their catalogue: Nazar boncuğu, Hamsa, Nimbu-mirchi, Ghanta, Drishti bommai, Páncháng jié,
Daruma, Maneki-neko, Horseshoe, Scarab, Himmeli, plus an emoji option.

Hangly ships every one of those except the emoji fallback — `nazar`, `hamsa`, `nimbuMirchi`,
`ghanta`, `drishtiBommai`, `panchangJie`, `daruma`, `manekiNeko`, `horseshoe`, `scarab`,
`himmeli` — and lists none of them on any page. They are in the 20-charm seasonal and lucky set
that belongs to no collection.

Screen Charms charges $4.99 for a Pro tier whose named charms are Turkey Nazar, Maneki Neko,
Daruma Doll, Evil Eye and Santa — four of which Hangly ships free. Desk Dangle's free ten
include Evil Eye, Nimbu Mirchi and Hamsa. Screen Dangle's ten talismans lead with Nazar.

**Every serious competitor in this category competes on the same dozen cultural luck charms.
Hangly has all of them, free, and is the only one not saying so.**

## Per competitor

Search demand is estimated from observable proxies — how many competitors target the term, what
appears in search results, and how much content each publishes against it. No keyword tool was
used and these are not volume figures.

### MenuBar Pets — **priority 1**

- **Demand:** Moderate and rising. Sits at the junction of two growing queries: menu bar
  customisation and desktop pets.
- **Feature overlap:** The highest of any app here. 12+ characters that *hang from the menu bar*,
  plus custom images from uploads. That is Hangly's exact mechanic and exact differentiator.
- **Intent overlap:** Near-total for "hanging character mac".
- **Authority opportunity:** High. A reader comparing the two finds nothing from us, and we have
  the stronger case — 75 charms against 12 hanging characters, free against $4.99 Pro, and cross
  platform against macOS only.
- **Page type:** Full comparison, `/compare/menubar-pets`.

### Book My Luck — **priority 2**

- **Demand:** Moderate, concentrated in India.
- **Feature overlap:** High. Same mechanic, same platforms, publishes a charm count (22).
- **Intent overlap:** High, and price-sensitive.
- **Authority opportunity:** High and unusually clean. They publish 22; we ship 75. They charge
  ₹99; we charge nothing. Both figures are verifiable, which makes for a comparison that reads as
  fact rather than claim.
- **Page type:** Full comparison, `/compare/book-my-luck`.

### Screen Charms — **priority 3**

- **Demand:** Moderate. Ranks well for "lucky charm mac menu bar".
- **Feature overlap:** High mechanic overlap, much smaller library. Notably includes a Pomodoro
  timer, which Hangly does not.
- **Intent overlap:** High.
- **Authority opportunity:** High. Their free tier is **one charm**; ours is 75. Custom images
  are locked behind $4.99; ours are free. This is the sharpest free-versus-paid contrast
  available, and it should be drawn without gloating — their Pomodoro timer is a real reason to
  choose them.
- **Page type:** Full comparison, `/compare/screen-charms`.

### Desk Dangle — **priority 4**

- **Demand:** Low to moderate, growing. Ships FAQPage schema, which most of this category does not.
- **Feature overlap:** High. Verlet physics, custom PNG, pauses in full-screen apps.
- **Intent overlap:** High for Windows users specifically.
- **Authority opportunity:** Moderate and **time-limited**. They are Windows-only with macOS
  planned. A comparison written now can honestly say "no Mac build today"; that advantage expires
  when they ship one, and the page will need revisiting rather than retiring.
- **Page type:** Comparison, `/compare/desk-dangle`.

### Drishti Dangle — **priority 5**

- **Demand:** Low, concentrated in India, but highly specific.
- **Feature overlap:** Moderate. Eight charms plus **four musical wind chimes**, which nothing
  else here offers.
- **Intent overlap:** Moderate. Overlaps Hangly's Tamil Divine and Protection collections.
- **Authority opportunity:** Moderate. The honest answer is that if you want wind chimes, they
  are the only option — and saying so plainly builds more credibility than another win column.
- **Page type:** Comparison, `/compare/drishti-dangle`. Already covered in guides; a page is
  optional.

### Lucky Dangle — **already covered, needs rewriting**

- **Authority opportunity:** **High, and currently unrealised.** Our existing page was written
  before we knew their price or catalogue. It says "See their site" where it could say $7.77, and
  it never mentions that eleven of their twelve charms are ones we give away.
- **Action:** Rewrite `/compare/lucky-dangle` with the real pricing and the catalogue overlap.
  This is the single highest-value edit to an existing page on the site.

### Screen Dangle — **already covered, minor update**

- Free, ten talismans, studio-style. Our page is broadly right. Add the ten-talisman figure.

### DangleJoy — **already covered, needs correcting**

- Our page records price and platforms as "See their site". They are **$4.99 lifetime, macOS 13+,
  Windows and Android, 30+ charms**. They are the second-largest library in the category and our
  page does not say so.
- **Action:** Correct the entry. Accuracy here matters more than the ranking.

### Charmly — **already covered, lowest value**

- Distributed through Glaze, publishes almost nothing. Our page carries "Not published" in most
  rows, which is honest but not compelling. It is also our weakest page by inbound links (2).
- **Consider:** folding this into the charm apps guide rather than maintaining a page built on
  unknowns.
