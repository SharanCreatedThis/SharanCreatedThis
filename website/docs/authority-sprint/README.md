## The three findings that shape everything else

**1. Hangly gives away what competitors sell.** Lucky Dangle charges $7.77–$11.11 for twelve
cultural charms. Eleven of them — nazar, hamsa, nimbu-mirchi, ghanta, drishti bommai, páncháng
jié, daruma, maneki-neko, horseshoe, scarab, himmeli — ship free in Hangly. They are in the
20-charm seasonal set that is listed on no page. Screen Charms puts four of the same behind a
$4.99 tier from a one-charm free version.

**2. Eight of the eleven collections are licensed IP.** Marvel, DC, BTS, Stranger Things,
Breaking Bad, Friends, plus named footballers and musicians. Collection landing pages for these
would be the highest-traffic pages on any content plan and are excluded from all of them. A charm
inside an app is not the same as an indexed page optimised for someone else's trademark.

**3. The remaining gap is not SEO.** It is that the product's strongest facts have no page, and
the domain has no inbound links. The first is a month of surfacing work. The second cannot be
fixed with code at all.
