# 6 — Execution roadmap

_Ranked by expected authority gain per unit of effort. Vision, appcasts and release
infrastructure are out of scope throughout._

## The thesis in one paragraph

Hangly has the largest charm library in its category, is free where most rivals are paid, and is
the only app shipping a native Windows ARM64 build — and none of that is arguable from a page,
because the charm library has no page and twenty of its best charms are listed nowhere. The
technical work is done. **The remaining gap is not SEO. It is that the product's strongest facts
are invisible and the domain has no inbound links.**

## What would make this fail

Three failure modes, named so they can be watched for.

**Building the eleven collection pages.** Eight of them target licensed IP — Marvel, DC, BTS,
Stranger Things, Breaking Bad, Friends, plus named footballers and musicians. They are the
highest-apparent-traffic pages on any list and the reason they are absent from every tier above.
The risk is not a ranking penalty; it is a takedown affecting the app.

**Building charm pages that are grids with two sentences.** The rule is 600 honest words or no
page. About 12 of the 75 charms clear that bar. Building 75 produces doorway pages and would
undo the quality position the site has spent this whole project establishing.

**Launching before the shop window is ready.** Product Hunt and Show HN are one-shot. Sending
that traffic to a product page listing 55 of 75 charms wastes the single largest authority
opportunity available.

---

## What to measure at 30, 90 and 180 days

- **30d:** does `/charms` get crawled and indexed? Do the awesome-mac listings appear?
- **90d:** do the cultural charm pages earn impressions for non-app queries — "what is a drishti
  bommai" rather than "desktop charm app"? That is the signal that the authority play is working.
- **180d:** are there inbound links from domains that are not directories? That is the only
  metric that moves authority from 5/10, and everything else here is in service of it.
