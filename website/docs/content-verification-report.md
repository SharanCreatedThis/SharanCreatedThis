## How to re-verify

1. Our own product figures are generated at build time by `scripts/generate-stats.mjs` and `scripts/generate-changelog.mjs`. They cannot drift; if one is wrong the source data is wrong.
2. Competitor claims carry a check date on the page they appear on. Re-read the source URL and update both the claim and `lastChecked` in `src/lib/verification/claims.ts`.
3. Star counts, prices and version numbers move. Anything older than about 90 days should be treated as low confidence regardless of what this table says.
