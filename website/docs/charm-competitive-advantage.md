**Eight of the eleven overlapping charms are invisible or near-invisible on Hangly's own site.**

### Screen Charms — 4 of ~5 overlap
Turkey Nazar, Maneki Neko, Daruma Doll, Evil Eye all ship in Hangly. Santa does not. Three of the
four are invisible on our site.

### Desk Dangle — 3 of 10 overlap
Evil Eye, Nimbu Mirchi, Hamsa. Their other seven — Banana Cat, Ruby Cherries, Pink Heart, Chonky
Cat, Fluffy Kitten, Pink Cassette, Orange Cat — are a different aesthetic entirely: cute and
contemporary rather than cultural. **That is a genuine gap in Hangly's catalogue, not a weakness
in theirs.**

### DangleJoy — partial overlap, and they have charms we do not
Dreamcatcher, Maneki Neko, Daruma overlap. **Omamori, Lucky Knot, Thai Bell, Dragon and Buddha do
not ship in Hangly.** DangleJoy's cultural catalogue is broader in East and Southeast Asian
charms than ours.

## Two corrections to existing site claims

1. **`entries.ts` records Book My Luck's price as "See their site".** It is ₹99 once, ₹297 for
   five, with 22 charms — read from their site on 2026-09-25.
2. **`entries.ts` records DangleJoy's price and platforms as "See their site".** They are $4.99
   lifetime, macOS 13+, Windows and Android, 30+ charms. They are the second-largest catalogue in
   the category and the site does not say so.

## The strategic read

The competitive position is strong and almost entirely unstated. The single highest-value action
is not a new argument — it is publishing the charms that already exist, because **eight of the
eleven charms that prove the strongest claim cannot currently be seen by anyone.**

Until `/charms/lucky` exists, the Lucky Dangle comparison cannot credibly assert the overlap: a
reader who clicks through to verify finds nothing.
