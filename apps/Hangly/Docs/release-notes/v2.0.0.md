# Hangly 2.0

A charm hangs from the top of your screen, on a rope, and swings.

That is the whole app. There is no window to keep open, no account, nothing to
sign in to. It lives in the menu bar, stays out of the way, and is there when you
look up.

**New in 2.0:** five cords instead of one, up to three charms on the same rope,
eleven seasonal charms that arrive on their own, weather, and one Customize
window in place of the three it used to take.

---

## Tiny hanging charms from cultures around the world

Twenty-seven charms to collect, drawn rather than photographed, each one a real
object that people actually hang: a Turkish nazar boncuğu against the evil eye, a
hamsa, an Indian nimbu-mirchi and drishti bommai, a Chinese pánchángjié knot, a
Japanese daruma and maneki-neko, a horseshoe, an Egyptian scarab, a Finnish
himmeli. Each comes with where it is from and what it is for.

## It hangs like a real thing

Push the charm and it swings, and keeps swinging, and slows down the way something
on a string slows down. Throw it and it carries. A heavy bell hangs steeper than a
straw ornament and settles sooner. Nothing about it is an animation playing back.

The rope even keeps the time of day: a swing holds a little longer in the morning
than it does at night.

## Hang up to three

Put one charm on the rope, or three, in whatever order you like, each at its own
size. They knock about on the same cord and a loaded rope hangs straighter and
swings slower.

## Five cords

Thread, leather, gold chain, silver chain and neon — and they are five different
ropes, not five colours. Gold swings slowly and heavily. Leather is stiff and
settles fast. Neon is the lightest and quickest. Change cords mid-swing and
nothing resets.

## Make your own

Drop any picture on it — a photo, a logo, your cat — and Hangly cuts the subject
out, works out how heavy it should be from its shape, and hangs it. It goes in
your Library beside the others.

## The weather, if you want it

Turned on, the charm takes on the sky: warmer in sun, drained under cloud, wet in
rain, frosted in snow, charged in a storm. It stays recognisably itself. **Off
until you ask for it**, and it never asks macOS where you are.

## Seasons arrive on their own

Eleven more charms in four sets — Halloween, Diwali, Christmas and New Year. A
season dresses the rope while it lasts and puts back what was there when it ends.
Choose a charm yourself and the season stands aside for the rest of the year. Turn
it off if you would rather it never happened.

## Out of the way when it matters

Hangly can take itself off the screen while a film is playing full screen and come
back when it ends. Off by default; turn it on in Appearance.

## One window

Library, Create, Appearance and About, in a sidebar. It replaced a tabbed
Settings, a Charm Library and an AI Studio — three places to learn before you
could change a charm. Closing it gives its memory back: Hangly idles at 23 MB
whether or not you have opened it.

## Yours

Hangly asks for **no permissions at all**. It cannot see your screen, your files,
your location or what you type.

Anonymous usage — which features get used, on which version of macOS — can be
shared to help decide what to build next, and it is a single switch in About.
It never includes your charms, your pictures, your files or anything you typed.
Turn it off and the identifier is deleted.

## Requirements

- macOS 14 Sonoma or later
- Apple Silicon (M1 or later)

## Installing

Open the disk image and drag Hangly to Applications.

Hangly is not yet signed with an Apple Developer ID, so macOS will refuse to open
it the first time. Right-click the app and choose **Open**, once. After that it
opens normally.

## Upgrading from 1.0

Drag the new copy over the old one. Your settings, your favourites and every
charm you have imported are read as they are — the settings document from 1.0
decodes unchanged, and anything 2.0 added takes its default. Nothing to migrate
and nothing to re-import.

## Known limitations

- Not notarised yet, so the first launch needs the right-click → Open step above.
- Weather needs a network connection for its first reading; after that it keeps
  wearing the last one it got.
- On macOS 26, large icon sizes are drawn inside the system's icon container,
  which double-frames an icon that has its own rounded background.
